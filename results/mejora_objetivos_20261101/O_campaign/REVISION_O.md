# Independent review of the O-campaign (2026-11-01)

Reviewer did not take part in designing, running or writing the campaign. Scope: protocol order, nested-selection integrity, speaker crossing, reproduction of reported figures, V0 comparator, promotion rule, fold 5, overclaims. Review scripts are not part of the repository (kept in the session scratchpad); every number below was recomputed from the stored files unless stated. EXPLORATORY campaign; this review confirms process and arithmetic, not scientific validity.

## Verdict

**The process and the arithmetic hold.** Protocol precedes results, no selection used the outer fold, no speaker crosses train/test, the reported F1, CIs, paired deltas and Holm values reproduce, the promotion rule was applied literally, fold 5 was used once and last. **Several statements in `README_O.md` sections 4-5 are overstated or wrong** (ceiling attribution, "early gains small", the 13/15 calibration count, the repetition promotion reading, two wording limits) and are listed below with the corrected wording. `README_O.md` itself was not edited by the reviewer. The promotion result ("criteria met") is correct under the frozen rule but is mostly a statement about a weak default, not about a strong pipeline. Nothing is confirmed; every figure stays exploratory.

## PLAUSIBLE (consistent with the evidence, not independently settled)

- **Non-winning trials are also computed on outer-train only.** I replayed and perturbed only the 15 winners; the other ~440 trial values come from the same `inner_oof` function whose tests and code (indexing by `tr`) show no outer access, but I did not recompute them.
- **Repetition weights.** `w` is the inverse-sampling weight of the frozen ASR-recovery frame, computed on the whole frame including rows later used as outer test. If the strata are defined by design variables (not by labels) this is harmless; I did not audit how the frame was drawn.
- **Selection optimism is small** (inner minus outer F1: filler 0.018, prolongation 0.007, repetition 0.038); consistent with the nested design, but the inner objective picks the threshold on its own scores for every configuration, so it is only comparable across configurations, not an unbiased estimate.
- **Zero-shot results are not informative either way.** EPIC (136 units, 29 positive speakers): F1 0.727 vs V0 0.721, delta +0.006 [-0.050, 0.068]. AMERESCO (98 units, 4 recordings): F1 0.534 [0.154, 0.759], precision 0.37, recall 0.95 (35/59/2) vs V0 0.269; CI spans zero. The packaged pipelines were not re-run by me on these corpora.
- **Stability.** 5/5 different winners in filler and repetition (modal 1/5): several configurations are near-equivalent; the reported figure is for a family, not for one specification.

## Overstated or refuted statements (corrections requested)

1. **"Ceiling is of features/labels/ASR, not of data volume or model family" (README_O section 5) - not supported as written.** The learning curve varies only the training fraction of one fixed winning configuration. It cannot attribute the ceiling to features, labels or ASR, and "LR and HGB tie" was not tested as a comparison (winners alternate; no paired LR-vs-HGB test). The 75% to 100% step adds only 33% more speakers, and for repetition its CI is [-0.033, +0.025]: a gain up to +0.025 is not excluded. Reading rule result, stated correctly: prolongation (+0.006 [-0.004, 0.016]) satisfies the frozen rule ("not data-limited with this model/features"); repetition satisfies it formally but with a wide CI; **filler does not satisfy the rule's wording** (CI excludes 0, point -0.014; the fixed configuration was selected on the 100% rows, which can create a non-monotone curve). Use: "no evidence of further gain from more training speakers with this configuration".
2. **"Early gains (25% to 75%) are small" - refuted for repetition** (+0.074 [0.024, 0.130]) and filler/prolongation gains of +0.025-0.028 are CI-excluding-zero. The curve rises up to 75%, then flattens.
3. **Repetition in the promotion reading.** The rule is met (all three classes superior to V0), but for repetition the optimised pipeline is not better than the one-line rule on P1 text (-0.002) and is nominally below S3 `ctx_LR` A2 (-0.030, Holm 0.095, not demonstrated). README_O says this already; downstream documents must not present "promotion criteria met" for repetition as an improvement over the best known route. Filler (+0.036 over the lexical rule) and prolongation (+0.027 over the S3 stack) are modest and come with lower precision (filler 0.74 vs rule 0.92).
4. **"Calibration chosen was none in 13/15 folds" - refuted (arithmetic).** The stored `stability.calibration` gives none 4/5 in each class (platt 1 in filler and prolongation, isotonic 1 in repetition) = **12/15**. The manifest correctly says `calibration_claim: none`; do not read the coverage/risk numbers as calibrated probabilities.
5. **Repetition "always-absent risk".** Recomputed weighted always-absent risk = 0.063 (positive share 214/3413). The S3 documents quote 0.043. I did not find the source of 0.043. Either value is below 0.10, so the conclusion "the risk criterion is not informative for repetition" stands; quote one number with its definition.
6. **Filler coverage/risk differs from S3 (0.932 / 0.097 [0.083, 0.110]) because it is a different system.** For the optimised filler pipeline the point values are 0.938 / 0.059 with the risk upper bound 0.068 (<= 0.10) on DIHANA marks. Wording allowed: "the original numeric thresholds (coverage >= 0.90, risk <= 0.10) are met by the optimised filler pipeline on DIHANA corpus marks". Not allowed: "OE4 indicator met" (the indicator needs double human annotation; the marks are not it; scores are not calibrated; abstention band chosen on DIHANA itself).

## Remaining limits no review could remove

DIHANA only (telephone, peninsular Spanish, 8 kHz); marks are corpus labels, not double human annotation; all corpora inspected in earlier phases; search budget never exhausted (patience stopped every search, so 22-57 of 60 configurations were tried out of 49-308); comparator V0 is the weak default; the dry run with budget 3 (18:16) printed one delta before the full launch (not used for any choice, declared in annex 1).

Tools used by the reviewer for 3-4 and part of 5: the campaign's own `mrcd.optimize` functions (so they test consistency of stored results with the code, not the code against an external truth); items 5 (F1, CI, delta, Holm) and 6 were reimplemented independently.
