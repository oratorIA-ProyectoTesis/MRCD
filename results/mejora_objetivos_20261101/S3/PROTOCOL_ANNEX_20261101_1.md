# Annex 1 to PROTOCOL.md (2026-11-01). PROTOCOL.md is unchanged (sha256 in PROTOCOL.sha256.json).

1. **Engine words (scope extension granted by the coordinator).** `DisfluencyEngine.analyze(..., return_words=False)` was added (default off; golden V0/P0 output bit-identical, `tests/test_s3_engine_words.py`). The service uses it only when `text_route` is requested. **Unit decision:** the opt-in text route applies the turn-level model to ASR *segments*: the word stream is cut at pauses >= 0.6 s (or 30 words). This unit differs from the DIHANA turn used for training and evaluation; the segment-level behaviour is NOT evaluated (no time-aligned reference exists). Declared extrapolation.
2. **E1 units.** The frame has 1025 units; 1019 are label-defined for `rep_literal` (6 positives of `revision` only have an indeterminate repetition label and are excluded, as in the parent protocol). 214 positives, 84 positive speakers, 122 speakers.
3. **Service calibrator.** The protocol did not fix which calibrator the service ships. Platt was chosen (two parameters, smaller artifact, ECE within the isotonic one in E3). The artifact (`mrcd/service/experimental/decision_bands_s3.json`) is fitted on all pooled development scores of folds 1-4 (raw scores for V0; OOF scores for the learned text model) with the band rule of PROTOCOL section 4. It is never labelled a calibrated probability (`calibration_claim: "none"`).
4. **Service route.** The text route integrated is A1 alone (no OR-fusion): E2 selected `t_or = never` for repetition and filler, i.e. fusion collapsed to the rule. The route artifact (`rep_ctx_p1_route.json`) is trained on the whole frame of P1 text (folds 1-4, weights). Filler fusion is not integrated (no gain over the lexical rule).
5. **E2 family.** The four tests of the family (two repetition tests computed in `run_e1.py`, two filler tests in `run_e2.py`) are corrected jointly in `run_e2.py`; the p-values reported in `e1/results.json` for `family_E2_rep_partial` are uncorrected.
6. **Prolongation fusion** is offline only: observable-lengthening features need ASR word times, and the service fusion would need a second ASR pass; not integrated. The service marks prolongation events as "observable lengthening; disfluent function not demonstrated".
7. **ECE wording rule** applied as written: "calibration improvement shown" only if the speaker-bootstrap CI of ECE(calibrated) - ECE(raw) is entirely below 0.
8. No feature, grid, comparator or threshold rule was changed after seeing results.
