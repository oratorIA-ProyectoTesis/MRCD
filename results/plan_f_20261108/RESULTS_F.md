## Limits (protocol section 8, restated)

One corpus (telephone, peninsular Spanish, train-information domain) for training and evaluation; the 36 evaluated speakers are disjoint from the FT training speakers but domain, channel and vocabulary are shared, so every FT gain is an in-domain upper bound. The Plan B val metrics were seen before this protocol (not an untouched cell). The shipped o2026_11 heads were fitted on these speakers (in-sample, biased in their favour). Turn-level scoring; the filler reference rewards emitting any non-lexical filler, not its form. The model weights are not published. `o2026_13` stays opt-in whatever the outcome.
